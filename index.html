Move to Make it Real
Following the L2 review for Hospitality 0901, the majority of rejected orders are due to rigid hands and arms. To prevent further rejections, all collectors must follow this Pre-Collection Readiness Routine.
🎯 The Goal
To eliminate rigidity and ensure all recorded movements are fluid, natural, and aligned with the task goals, ensuring a higher first-pass acceptance rate. We must shift from "mechanical" movements to "natural" human gestures.
1
The Huddle Warm-up
Physical Preparation
  • Shoulder Rolls: 10 rotations backward, 10 forward.
  • Arm Circles: Wide, slow circles to loosen the joints.
  • Wrist & Finger Stretch: Gentle rotations and "finger flicks" to ensure hand agility.
  • Reach & Stretch: Full arm extensions to shake off rigidity.
📋 During the daily huddle session, prior to entering the collection area, all collectors will perform a 2-minute Mobility Flow. This reduces physical tension and "stiffness."
2
Task Rehearsal
Cognitive Preparation
  • Dry Run: Perform the specific task goal 2–3 times without recording.
  • Visualization: Visualize the most natural way a person would perform this action in real life.
  • SME Validation: If there is any doubt, demonstrate the movement to your SME for a "Green Light" before recording.
💡 Pro Tip: In hospitality, gestures are welcoming and soft. Avoid "robotic" or "stiff" positioning. If you feel tense, stop, shake out your arms, and try again. Physical looseness must be paired with mental clarity.
